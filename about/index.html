About Wacky's

Cabin by day.Arcade by night.

A Northern Ontario cedar-log cabin serving hand-tossed wings in over 100 flavours, a full bar and an arcade under one roof.

Our story

Born in the Soo, December 2007

Wacky's opened its doors in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario in December 2007 with a simple idea: the best wings in the North, more flavours than anyone thought possible, and a room where the whole family could hang out.

Walk in and you're in a cabin — walls covered in Northern white cedar logs, custom wooden tables, chairs and bar tops — with big screens on every wall and the glow of the game room around the corner.

Today there are 5 Wacky's across Northern Ontario, still tossing wings by hand in over 100 flavours.
